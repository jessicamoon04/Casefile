      2. Buyer: open a dispute

      1. Go to sandbox.paypal.com and log in with the same sandbox buyer account.
      2. Open Resolution Center and report a problem with this payment (for example, item not received).
      3. Come back to Casefile. It checks PayPal every minute, so the new dispute appears on its own under Your disputes. PayPal's sandbox can take a few minutes to make a new dispute available.
