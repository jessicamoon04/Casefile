What Casefile is

Casefile helps sellers who accept PayPal payments answer disputes. It reads your disputes and orders from PayPal, uses an AI agent to investigate them, drafts a response, and sends what you approve to PayPal. It is a prototype built for the PayPal AI Hackathon, intended for PayPal's sandbox environment. Features may change or stop working, and we may change or close the service at any time.

Casefile is not affiliated with or endorsed by PayPal or Anthropic. PayPal decides the outcome of every dispute.
