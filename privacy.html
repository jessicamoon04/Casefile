Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

The short version:

  • Casefile reads your PayPal disputes, payments and orders through PayPal's API, so it can show and investigate your cases.
  • When you start an investigation, the case details, the records you pasted and the documents you uploaded are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis.
  • Casefile has no database. Your investigations, notes, notifications, settings and uploaded documents are kept in your own browser.
  • Nothing is sent to PayPal on your behalf until you approve it.
  • Casefile sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising.

Who we are

Casefile is run by Big Chi. If you have a question about this policy or your data, email jotesbychicollections@gmail.com.

Casefile is a prototype built for the PayPal AI Hackathon. It is meant to be used with PayPal's sandbox (test) environment. Casefile is not affiliated with or endorsed by PayPal or Anthropic.

Information Casefile handles

From PayPal, once you connect your account: your disputes (reason, amount, status, deadlines and the messages posted in them), the payments and orders behind them (items, amounts, the shipping address and any tracking stored on the order), and the buyer's name as PayPal provides it.

From you: records you paste (such as tracking events, emails and policies), documents you upload (photos, PDFs and text files), internal notes, findings you flag, the drafts you edit and approve, your store name and your settings.

Access code: if the site is protected with an access code, the code you enter is kept in your browser for that tab only, and sent with each request so the server can check it.

Your buyers' information

Disputes include personal information about your buyers, such as their names, shipping addresses and messages. You decide which disputes Casefile works on, so you are responsible for that information under the law that applies to you. Casefile only uses it to show, investigate and answer the dispute you open. A buyer with a question about their information should contact the seller first, or email us at the address above.

The AI investigation (Anthropic)

When you click Start investigation or request a reassessment, Casefile sends Anthropic's Claude API the case it is working on: the PayPal dispute and order details, your pasted records, the documents you uploaded for that case, and, if you choose to include them, your internal notes and flagged findings. Nothing is sent to Anthropic until you start an investigation. Anthropic processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

AI findings can be wrong. Casefile labels each finding as verified, seller-provided, AI interpretation, unverified or missing, and checks quoted text against its source, but you should review everything before relying on it.

PayPal

Casefile reads your dispute data from PayPal and, only when you approve it, sends PayPal the evidence, offers, messages or decisions you chose. PayPal handles that data under its own privacy statement (opens in a new tab).

The sandbox demo store loads PayPal's checkout script from PayPal's servers so you can make test payments. PayPal may set its own cookies on that page. The rest of Casefile only loads files from our own site.

Where your information is kept

In your browser: investigations, run history, case activity, notes, notifications, settings and sample-case progress are saved in your browser's local storage, and uploaded documents in its IndexedDB storage. They stay on that device until you clear them. You can remove all of them at once in Casefile under Preferences, Clear saved data, or by clearing your browser's site data.

On our server: Casefile's server functions handle each request and then discard it. They don't keep copies of your disputes, documents or investigations. Error logs may record a short error message and PayPal's debug ID, but not the contents of your cases.

Hosting: Casefile is hosted by Vercel (opens in a new tab), which automatically processes technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Vercel's privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

Who we share it with

Only the providers named on this page: PayPal (to read and answer disputes), Anthropic (to run investigations you start) and Vercel (hosting). We don't sell information or use it for advertising. These providers may process data outside Nigeria, including in the United States.

Cookies and tracking

Casefile does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It uses your browser's storage only for the features described above. The demo store is the one page where PayPal's own script may set cookies.

Why we can handle this information

We handle this information to provide the service you ask for: showing, investigating and answering your disputes. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and the UK or EU GDPR where they apply, we rely on performing that service for you and on our legitimate interest in running a working, secure service.

Children

Casefile is for businesses and people aged 18 or over. It is not meant for children.

Your rights

Most of your Casefile data is in your own browser, where you can delete it at any time. For data held by PayPal, Anthropic or Vercel, their policies explain your rights. If you have a question or request, email jotesbychicollections@gmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (opens in a new tab) or the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Casefile handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
