Built for small sellers who take PayPal. Casefile reads the dispute, the order and your records, checks every buyer claim against the evidence, and drafts your response. You approve before anything is sent.

Open Casefile

How it works

  1. Investigate. A Claude agent works through six stages: the PayPal dispute, the order, shipping, conversations, store policies and your documents.
  2. Decide. Every buyer claim is set against the evidence, and Casefile recommends fighting, refunding or a fair offer.
  3. Approve. You edit the drafted response, see exactly what PayPal will receive, then send it.

Safety

  1. Nothing is sent without you. The agent can only read; every action needs your approval.
  2. Quotes are checked. Every excerpt the AI cites is matched word for word against the source.
  3. Honest statuses. Casefile says what it couldn't check, such as live carrier tracking, instead of guessing.
